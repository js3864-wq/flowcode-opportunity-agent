## Ranked opportunities

### 1. Race-week fan capture: live, routable codes on every screen across the three connected zones (score 89)

**Act now:** Race week, Nov 19-21 (screen content only, no print). **Pitch to:** Marketing and fan experience leads at Las Vegas Grand Prix, Inc.. **Unit:** Race weekend.

**What's happening.** The race runs Nov 19-21 and, for the first time, all three fan zones are connected, so fans move freely between the Koval Zone by Heineken, East Harmon and West Harmon. The first official F1 Afterparty at Sphere follows on Nov 21. [1][2][3][4][5][6][7]

**Why Flowcode.** Codes on digital screens need no print run, so they can go live this week. FlowID ties each scan to a fan the promoter owns, and routing by zone and time lets one code sell Afterparty add-ons on Friday and capture 2027 presale sign-ups after the race. C3 Presents drove 1.3M+ scans across its 2024 festivals with this pattern. Proof: C3 Presents (ACL, Lollapalooza, Bonnaroo), 1.3M+ audience scans across its 2024 festivals (https://www.flowcode.com/customer-stories/c3-presents).

**The play.**
1. Run one Flowcode per zone on fan-zone and grandstand screens, so movement between the three zones becomes measurable.
2. Route by time: Afterparty add-on before Saturday's race, merch during sessions, 2027 presale and locals offers after the checkered flag.
3. Capture opt-in with FlowID on a FlowHub and sync to the promoter's CRM daily during race week.
4. Send each zone sponsor a scan report the Monday after the race.

**Products:** Flowcodes, FlowHubs, FlowID & Personalization, Analytics & Integrations. **KPIs:** Opt-ins captured per zone, Afterparty add-on revenue from scans, 2027 presale sign-ups.
**Score:** timeliness 5, fit 5, scale 5, evidence 5, access 2.
**Caveats:** Two days is very short for procurement. If race week is missed, lead the 2027 pitch with this year's gap. Zones carry sponsor naming (Heineken, T-Mobile), so codes may need sponsor sign-off. F1 already runs its own fan-zone QR draws through F1 Unlocked. Position Flowcode as the promoter's identity layer, not a competing prize draw.

### 2. Turn Grand Prix Plaza visits into 2027 race tickets (score 80)

**Next 90 days:** Before the Plaza's 2027 season opens. **Pitch to:** General manager, Grand Prix Plaza. **Unit:** Grand Prix Plaza.

**What's happening.** Grand Prix Plaza, billed as North America's largest F1 attraction, reopened Jan 30, 2026 with an upgraded 4D theater, a new karting layout and new event spaces. Zoox will run robotaxi rides to the Plaza from 2027, and the Plaza offers locals discounts after earlier pushback on ticket prices. [8][9][10][11][12][13]

**Why Flowcode.** People who karted or watched the 4D show in spring are the warmest 2027 ticket buyers. Public sources show no link between a Plaza visit and a race ticket, so this is worth validating. Codes on karting, theater and retail touchpoints feed a FlowHub with locals pricing and presale access, and analytics show which attraction converts. The Penguins saw 88% conversion from in-venue prompts. Proof: Pittsburgh Penguins, 88% conversion rate and $8,000 in revenue in 30 minutes from in-arena live reads (https://www.flowcode.com/customer-stories/pittsburgh-penguins).

**The play.**
1. Place a distinct Flowcode at karting check-in, the 4D theater exit and retail.
2. Route locals to locals pricing and visitors to 2027 presale, with FlowID capturing who scanned.
3. Add a code at the Zoox pickup zone when rides start in 2027.
4. Report Plaza-to-ticket conversion monthly.

**Products:** Flowcodes, FlowHubs, FlowID & Personalization, Analytics & Integrations. **KPIs:** Plaza visitors captured, Plaza-to-race-ticket conversion, Locals sign-ups.
**Score:** timeliness 4, fit 5, scale 3, evidence 5, access 4.
**Caveats:** The Plaza's 2027 opening date and visitor numbers were not found in sources.

3. Standardize placement and reporting so the playbook can be copied to Miami and Austin.

### 5. Trade general admission fans up to hospitality (score 70)

**Act now:** Race week, then the 2027 on-sale. **Pitch to:** Head of ticketing and hospitality sales. **Unit:** Ticketing and hospitality.

**What's happening.** Prices run from $50 single days to $28,885 for Gordon Ramsay at F1 Garage, and single-day hospitality launched at the 100-days-out mark to lower the entry point. [14][15][16][5][1][2]

**Why Flowcode.** General admission and grandstand fans are on site and within sight of the clubs. A code on their screens leading to next-day or 2027 single-day hospitality, tied to FlowID, shows who is ready to trade up, and CRM sync lets sales follow up after the race.

**The play.**
1. Put codes on general admission and grandstand screens promoting next-day or 2027 single-day hospitality.
2. Personalize the FlowHub by zone and ticket type.
3. Push high-intent scans to the hospitality sales queue in the CRM.
4. Measure trade-up rate by zone.

**Products:** Flowcodes, FlowHubs, FlowID & Personalization, Analytics & Integrations. **KPIs:** Hospitality leads from scans, Trade-up revenue, Lead-to-sale rate.
**Score:** timeliness 4, fit 4, scale 3, evidence 5, access 3.
**Caveats:** Hospitality for this weekend may already be sold out. Fall back to the 2027 presale.
