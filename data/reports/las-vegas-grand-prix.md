**With 100,000+ fans a day arriving Thursday and the race locked in through 2037, Las Vegas can use this weekend to start the 2027 buyer list, track sponsor results and sell merch, on its own data.**

- F1 extended the Las Vegas Grand Prix through 2037 (June 4), and the LVCVA approved a $100M, 10-year sponsorship. Fixed race-week assets now pay off over a decade.
